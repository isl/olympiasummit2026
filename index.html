I

ABOUT THE SUMMIT

Where history and innovation meet

It is our pleasure to invite you to the Olympia AI Data Infrastructure Summit 2026, hosted by FORTH in Heraklion, Crete, on 4–5 November 2026.

The summit will bring together leading researchers, engineers, and industry pioneers from Europe and around the world to exchange insights on agent memory, enterprise ontology, semantic data infrastructure, knowledge engineering, and AI-native application architectures.

We look forward to welcoming you to Crete, where history and innovation meet, to explore the technologies shaping the future of enterprise intelligence.
